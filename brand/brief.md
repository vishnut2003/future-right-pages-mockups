## Audiences

Two separate journeys that must not be mixed:

1. **Clients ("Plan My Future"):** young families, tech professionals, business owners, pre-retirees.
2. **Recruits ("Join Our Team"):** people considering a career in financial services. No income promises.

- Client pages: "Book a free consultation" (sticky "Book a free call" and click-to-call on phones).

## Tone

Trustworthy, calm, premium, human, educational. Plain language, visible contact details, real-people photography.
No hype, no guarantees, no returns or income figures.
