   guarantee, which the compliance rules for this site do not allow. Needs a decision from Future Right's compliance
   contact.
2. **Service names.** The slide lists Financial & College Fund Planning, Alternate Investment, Tax Strategy,
   Retirement Planning, Estate Planning and Long-Term Care. The MoU and the services grid list Retirement Planning,
   Life & Family Protection, Kids Education Funding, Estate Planning, Tax-Smart Savings and Investment Planning. The
   video uses the slide's names; the grid uses the MoU's. Future Right should confirm which list is correct.
3. **The video is a preview.** It has no voiceover. Producing the final video (script, voiceover, export) is not in
   the MoU.
4. **Photos and the background clip** are licensed stock placeholders until Future Right supplies its own.
